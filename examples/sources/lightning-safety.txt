Lightning safety guidance starts with a simple rule: when thunder roars,
go indoors. A sturdy building or a hard-topped car is the safest place
during a storm. Open fields, hilltops, isolated trees, and water should be
avoided until 30 minutes after the last thunder is heard.

The distance rule used in safety guides counts the seconds between the
flash and the thunder and divides by five to get the distance in miles.
A ten second gap means the storm is about two miles away. If the gap is
30 seconds or less, the storm is close enough to be dangerous and shelter
should be sought at once.
